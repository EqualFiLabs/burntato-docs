Anyone may add ETH to the Winner reserve, Recovery reserve, or both for **any future round**. This supports announced promotional rounds without modifying a game already in progress.

## Funding entry points

```solidity
fundWinnerReserve(targetRoundId) payable
fundRecoveryReserve(targetRoundId) payable
fundRoundReserves(targetRoundId, winnerAmount, recoveryAmount) payable
```

The combined call requires `winnerAmount + recoveryAmount == msg.value`. At least one funded amount must be positive. Active and past targets revert.

Funding is additive, permissionless, available during the global pause, and irreversible.

## What sponsorship does not do

Funding a distant round does not:

- activate that round;
- alter its eventual price or percentage configuration;
- make a POTATO commitment for the sponsor;
- change the current round; or
- execute a buyback.

The named reserve becomes a live pot only when its round activates.

## Aggregate vs sponsored

`roundFunding(roundId)` returns:

```text
winnerReserve
recoveryReserve
winnerSponsoredEth
recoverySponsoredEth
```

The first pair are total pending reserves. The second pair include only explicit permissionless contributions. Subtracting sponsored from aggregate shows automatic protocol/game funding such as the genesis seed, first-Grab funding, and configured next-round allocation.

This distinction should be visible in analytics. Labeling all reserved ETH “sponsorship” misrepresents player-funded mechanics.

## Announcing a future round

A promotion can cite a target round and its onchain reserve. Participants should still verify current contract state: the funding is real, but the round's eventual activation time depends on the preceding games.
