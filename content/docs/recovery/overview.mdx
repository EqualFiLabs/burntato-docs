Recovery turns POTATO into a claim on a future contest rather than a simple
sell decision. Commit POTATO **before** the next round begins; when that round
settles, its Recovery ETH is divided by commitment weight.

## The simple model

If a round settles with 10 ETH in Recovery and 1,000,000 POTATO committed, an account that committed 100,000 POTATO has 10% of the weight. Before rounding and claim-order dust, its share is 1 ETH.

```text
ordinary claim = floor(recoveryPool * accountCommitment / totalCommitted)
```

The last outstanding claimant receives the exact remainder, closing the pool without stranded division dust.

## Commit forward, not backward

`commitRecovery(amount)` always targets `currentRoundId + 1`. The target round's terms are already snapshotted, but the target has not activated.

POTATO moves into Diamond escrow through an exact protocol transfer. An ERC-20 approval is not a way around the token's transfer restrictions, and the commitment is not a normal wallet transfer.

## What happens to committed POTATO

At target settlement, all committed POTATO is consumed once:

```text
treasuryPotato = floor(totalCommitted * recoveryTreasuryBps / 10_000)
burnedPotato = totalCommitted - treasuryPotato
```

The burn-as-remainder rule accounts for every base unit. At launch, 90% is
burned and 10% becomes claimable by Treasury.

## What sets the return

Recovery is not a fixed-price redemption. A participant's outcome depends on:

- the target round's Recovery ETH;
- total POTATO committed by everyone;
- the account's commitment; and
- the canonical market value and other uses of POTATO.

Committing removes the tokens from ordinary market use and exposes them to the target round's result.

## No-commitment rounds

If nobody commits, the Recovery ETH rolls forward to the next round. It is not sent to Treasury or trapped.
