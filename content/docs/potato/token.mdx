POTATO is an 18-decimal ERC-20 implemented behind the Burntato Diamond. It supports standard balances, allowances, and EIP-2612 Permit, but its transfer policy is intentionally narrower than a typical ERC-20.

## Supply sources

New POTATO can enter supply through base holder-time emission. Genesis also mints a separate configured allocation into Diamond custody for the canonical market. Treasury reward schedules distribute existing POTATO and do not mint.

Recovery burns, voluntary self-burning, and other protocol burns reduce total supply.

## Allowed movement

The transfer hook permits:

1. minting and burning;
2. transfers where either endpoint is an administered distributor;
3. transfers where either endpoint is the current protocol authority;
4. an exact protocol transfer authorized and consumed during a Diamond self-call; and
5. a PoolManager transfer covered by the canonical hook's exact transaction-scoped transient allowance.

All other underlying POTATO transfers revert with `TransferRestricted`.

:::warning Approval is not transfer permission
`approve()` and Permit can install an allowance, but they do not bypass the transfer policy. A downstream `transferFrom()` must still use an allowed path.
:::

## Self-burning

Any holder may call `burn(amount)`. This is the supported voluntary destruction path and reduces both the wallet balance and `totalSupply()` exactly.

## Market wrappers are different assets

Uniswap v4 ERC-6909 currency claims and third-party wrappers can represent POTATO exposure, but they are not underlying POTATO balances. Their transfers do not invoke POTATO's transfer hook and should not be presented as identical custody.

## Integration reads

Read `canonicalHook()`, `tokenPoolManager()`, `isDistributor(account)`, and `transientPoolManagerAllowance()` when diagnosing a movement. The transient allowance should be zero after a successful transaction.
