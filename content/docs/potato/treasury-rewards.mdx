Treasury reward schedules add a second holder-time budget to future rounds. They move existing POTATO into escrow; they do not mint new tokens or change base emission.

## Allocate a schedule

The current reward allocator calls:

```solidity
allocateTreasuryRewards(amount, firstRoundId, roundCount)
```

The first target must be later than the current activated round. The allocator must hold the exact POTATO, which moves through the Diamond's one-shot protocol-transfer path without a separate ERC-20 approval.

The schedule divides the amount into `perRound` plus a `firstRoundRemainder`, so every base unit is accounted for. Multiple schedules add together.

## Earn through the same time curve

An activated round tracks base emission and Treasury-funded emission separately. Both vest under the holder-time step and duration for that round, but the Treasury component transfers escrowed inventory instead of minting.

`currentEarnedEmission()` returns both base and Treasury-earned values. `Round` exposes separate budgets, opportunities, remaining amounts, emitted amounts, and released amounts.

## Cancel future allocations

The current allocator may cancel a schedule once while it still has unactivated value. Cancellation releases only the future amount into `treasuryPotatoAvailable()`; it does not send tokens directly or alter an active round.

Settlement similarly releases unused active-round Treasury reward inventory.

## Roles are independent

The reward allocator, Treasury recipient, and distributor registry are three separate controls. Changing one does not silently change the others.
