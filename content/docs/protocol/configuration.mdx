`ProtocolConfig` holds the game defaults used for future rounds:

```solidity
struct ProtocolConfig {
    uint256 startingPrice;
    uint16 priceIncreaseBps;
    uint256 roundTimeout;
    uint256 roundEmissionBudget;
    uint16 emissionStepBps;
    uint256 emissionVestingDuration;
    uint16 winnerBps;
    uint16 nextRoundWinnerBps;
    uint16 recoveryBps;
    uint16 treasuryBps;
    uint16 recoveryBurnBps;
    uint16 recoveryTreasuryBps;
    uint16 buybackBps;
    uint16 operatorPurchaseBps;
    uint256 roundTimeoutDecay;
    uint256 minimumRoundTimeout;
}
```

## Snapshot boundary

When Round N activates, it snapshots the complete current configuration for Round N+1. A governance update cannot rewrite the active round or the already-open next-round Recovery market. It affects later unsnapshotted rounds.

`Round.activated` is the lifecycle marker; a zero emission budget is valid and is not used as a sentinel.

## Validation

- All BPS values are at most 10,000.
- Winner, next-Winner, Recovery, Treasury, buyback, and Operator purchase shares sum to exactly 10,000.
- Recovery burn and Treasury shares sum to exactly 10,000.
- Starting price, initial timeout, minimum timeout, and vesting duration are nonzero.
- Minimum timeout cannot exceed initial timeout.
- Timeout decay cannot exceed initial timeout.
- Initial timeout fits the `uint64` deadline-safety bound.

Zero price growth, timeout decay, emission step, and round emission budget are allowed.

## Separate configuration domains

The hook fee and Operator fee share live in the hook, not `ProtocolConfig`. The installed hook caps the total fee at 200 BPS and allows 0–100% of that fee to route to Operators.

Buyback maximum spend, caller reward, and block delay live in `BuybackConfig`. The installed facet caps caller reward at 100 BPS.

Market dependencies and the POTATO seed are configurable only before market launch. Treasury recipient, distributor permissions, reward allocator, guardian, and hook ownership are independent controls.

## Displaying live economics

For the current game, read `getRound(currentRoundId).config`. For future defaults, read `protocolConfig()`. Do not label governed defaults as the active round's terms unless both values match.
