Burntato's safety depends on explicit accounting boundaries, snapshotted participant terms, constrained token movement, and narrowly scoped external integrations.

## Accounting invariants

- Every later-Grab split equals the exact purchase price; Treasury receives rounding dust.
- First-Grab ETH goes entirely to the following round's Winner reserve.
- A reserve is applied to its named round exactly once.
- Raw ETH does not silently become a Winner, Recovery, or buyback liability.
- Recovery consumes every committed POTATO base unit once.
- The final Recovery claimant receives remaining division dust.
- A holder opportunity can finalize only once.

## Participant-term invariants

- An active round uses its own `RoundConfig`.
- The next Recovery target is snapshotted before commitments open.
- Later governance changes apply only to unsnapshotted rounds.
- Stored deadlines and prices, rather than client reconstructions, are authoritative.

## Token and market invariants

- Ordinary underlying POTATO transfers revert.
- Protocol and PoolManager movement authorizations are exact and transaction-scoped.
- Only the canonical hook and PoolKey may operate the configured market path.
- Exact-output and zero-amount swaps are rejected.
- Launch position NFTs are irrecoverably sent to the dead address.
- Market launch is single-use and post-launch configuration is closed.

## Liveness controls

Permissionless settlement, reserve funding, buyback execution, and Operator synchronization reduce dependence on a privileged caller. Pull claims let recipients choose when and where to receive value.

The 30-day holderless-predecessor escape prevents Recovery commitments from remaining indefinitely stranded before a game ever begins. Once play starts, the ordinary settlement path is binding.

## Administrative assumptions

Before authority renunciation, users trust the configured authority for
selector upgrades and future economic settings. Hook ownership separately
controls fees, fee recipients, Operator routing, and the public-buy gate. On
Robinhood mainnet, PoolManager governance is external to Burntato.

Diamond finalization removes selector upgrades but not these other roles. Verify each address and state independently.

:::warning Verify what you use
Mainnet addresses, source verification, current roles, and published security
assessments should all match the official launch registry before you interact.
:::
