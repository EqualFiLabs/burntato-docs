Burntato presents one primary integration address: `BurntatoDiamond`. Gameplay, POTATO, Recovery, settlement, claims, market views, buybacks, Treasury rewards, and governance selectors route through this EIP-2535 Diamond.

## Diamond facets

| Facet | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| `DiamondCutFacet` | Add, replace, or remove selectors until finalized. |
| `DiamondLoupeFacet` | Inspect installed facets and ERC-165 support. |
| `GovernanceFacet` | Authority, guardian, pause, defaults, recipients, and purchase activation. |
| `GameFacet` | Rounds, Grabs, reserves, current emission, and purchase routing. |
| `RecoveryFacet` | Forward POTATO commitments, reserve funding, and stalled exits. |
| `SettlementFacet` | Expired-round settlement. |
| `ClaimsFacet` | Winner, Recovery, and Treasury pull claims. |
| `PotatoTokenFacet` | ERC-20, Permit, burn, distributors, and exact movement authorization. |
| `MarketFacet` | Canonical v4 market configuration, launch, curves, and PoolKey. |
| `BuybackFacet` | Buyback reserve, configuration, and canonical swaps. |
| `TreasuryRewardsFacet` | Pre-funded future reward schedules. |

Shared state lives in collision-resistant namespaced libraries. Integrators should call interfaces at the Diamond address, not facet implementations.

## External contracts

The **BurntatoSwapFeeHook** is a separate owned contract bound immutably to POTATO, its PoolManager, and tick spacing. It enforces canonical-pool rules, collects the bilateral fee, splits realized ETH, and controls external buys.

The **BurntatoOperatorRewardsRouter** is a separate immutable integration bound to the Burntato Diamond, Statics Operator NFT, and Activation Registry. It accounts for revenue outside Diamond storage.

Uniswap v4's PoolManager and PositionManager are external dependencies. On
Robinhood mainnet, Burntato validates and uses the canonical chain deployments.

## One address, multiple assets and roles

The Diamond is also the POTATO ERC-20 address and can custody POTATO and ETH liabilities. Internal accounting—not raw balance alone—distinguishes Winner, Recovery, Treasury, buyback, launch seed, commitments, and reward escrow.

## Upgrade boundary

Before finalization, the authority can change the selector table. `finalizeProtocol()` disables future Diamond cuts only. The hook, external PoolManager, Operator router, and defined economic administration have separate boundaries.
