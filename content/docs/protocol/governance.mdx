Burntato assigns one Diamond `authority`, which may be an EOA, Safe, or
governance contract. Hook ownership and canonical PoolManager governance are
separate roles.

## Diamond authority

While nonzero, authority can:

- modify Diamond selectors until finalization;
- update future protocol defaults;
- set the Diamond Treasury recipient and explicit POTATO distributors;
- configure buyback limits and the reward allocator;
- appoint or remove the guardian;
- pause or unpause;
- configure the market before launch; and
- transfer or safely relinquish authority.

Mainnet opens for play through the one-time `initializePurchases()` action after
deployment and launch checks complete.

## Guardian

The guardian is containment-only. It may change the global pause from false to true but cannot clear it. Only authority can unpause.

While paused, Burntato blocks:

- new Grabs and Recovery commitments;
- holder-emission materialization and protocol minting;
- round settlement; and
- Diamond Winner, Recovery, Treasury ETH, and Treasury POTATO claims.

The pause deliberately leaves views, permitted token actions, self-burning, eligible stalled-Recovery withdrawals, market swaps and launch, buybacks and reserve funding, reward scheduling, and administration available.

## Finalization

`finalizeProtocol()` permanently disables `diamondCut`. It does **not** freeze economics, clear the guardian, unpause, renounce authority, transfer hook ownership, or disable other defined administration.

This distinction should be explicit in governance interfaces: “Diamond cuts finalized” is accurate; “protocol immutable” is not.

## Safe authority renunciation

Authority can become zero only after purchases are initialized, guardian is zero, and the protocol is unpaused. The safe sequence is therefore:

1. initialize purchases;
2. remove guardian;
3. clear the pause; and
4. set authority to the zero address.

Independent hook and PoolManager ownership do not change automatically.
