Burntato is a timed ownership game played with ETH. One wallet holds the Hot Potato at a time. A **Grab** pays the displayed price, gives the Potato to the buyer, resets the countdown, and raises the price of the next Grab.

If nobody Grabs before the countdown expires, the final holder wins the round's **Winner pot**. Holding the Potato also creates an opportunity to earn **POTATO**, the protocol token used by the Recovery Market.

:::note The short version
Grab the Potato, hold it long enough to earn POTATO, and either pass it to the next player or remain the holder when time expires.
:::

## The core loop

1. **Grab:** pay the exact current price in ETH.
2. **Hold:** the countdown runs while your POTATO opportunity vests.
3. **Pass or win:** another player can Grab at the higher next price. If nobody does, the current holder can settle the round and claim the Winner pot.
4. **Recover:** POTATO holders can commit tokens before a future round starts, then share that round's Recovery ETH after settlement.

The game accounts for ETH on each purchase, but POTATO emission advances only with actual holder time. Rapid cycling does not create free emission.

## Where the ETH goes

The first Grab of every round funds the next round's Winner reserve. From the second Grab onward, the active round's snapshotted configuration divides the price among:

- the current Winner pot;
- the next round's Winner reserve;
- the current Recovery pool;
- Treasury;
- the buyback reserve; and
- Operator rewards, when configured.

See [Money flows](/docs/start/money-flows) for the accounting map and [Defaults](/docs/reference/defaults) for the fresh-deployment values.

## POTATO is more than a points token

POTATO is emitted to holders, used in Recovery, traded through one canonical Uniswap v4 market, and deliberately transfer-restricted. It can move through supported protocol and market paths, but ordinary wallet-to-wallet transfers revert.

The canonical market begins with 100 million POTATO spread across 56 permanently locked liquidity positions. Treasury buybacks place ETH into that market. External buys begin disabled on a fresh deployment and can be enabled later by hook governance.

## Three systems, one game

| System | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Hot Potato | Prices Grabs, runs the timer, allocates purchase ETH, and settles the Winner pot. |
| Recovery Market | Escrows forward POTATO commitments and distributes a target round's Recovery ETH. |
| Canonical market | Provides POTATO/ETH price discovery, Treasury fee revenue, and protocol-funded buybacks. |

Statics Operators add a fourth participation path: registered Operators share configured purchase revenue and part of the existing swap fee according to activation weight.
