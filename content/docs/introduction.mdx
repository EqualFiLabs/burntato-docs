Burntato is a fully onchain game of timing, conviction, and nerve. One wallet
holds the Hot Potato. Every **Grab** pays the displayed ETH price, passes the
Potato to a new holder, resets the countdown, and raises the price of the next
Grab.

If the countdown reaches zero, the final holder wins the round's **Winner
pot**. While the clock runs, holding the Potato earns **POTATO**, the asset used
to compete for ETH in the Recovery Market.

:::note The short version
Grab the Potato, hold it to earn POTATO, and decide whether the next player
takes it from you before the clock runs out.
:::

## One action drives the whole system

1. **Grab:** pay the exact current price and become the holder.
2. **Hold:** your POTATO opportunity vests with real holder time.
3. **Pass or win:** another player can pay the higher next price, or the timer
   can expire with you still holding.
4. **Recover:** commit POTATO before the next round and compete for its Recovery
   ETH.

Rapid cycling cannot manufacture emission. A fast pass earns little or no
POTATO; a full four-minute hold earns the complete opportunity available to
that holder.

## Grabs build more than one prize

The first Grab funds the following round's Winner pot. From Grab two onward,
launch economics send 25% to the current Winner pot, 2% to the next Winner pot,
40% to Recovery, 13% to POTATO buybacks, 15% to Statics Operators, and 5% to
Treasury.

That means 67% of each later Grab builds current or future player pots, while
the buyback allocation connects game activity to the POTATO market.

See [Money flows](/docs/start/money-flows) for the complete path and [Mainnet
launch parameters](/docs/reference/launch-parameters) for every launch value.

## POTATO connects play, Recovery, and the market

POTATO is earned through holder time, committed and largely burned through
Recovery, and priced in one canonical native ETH/POTATO Uniswap v4 pool. Its
underlying token is deliberately transfer-restricted: protocol actions and the
canonical market can move it, while ordinary wallet-to-wallet transfers
revert.

The market launches with 100 million POTATO distributed across 56 permanently
locked liquidity positions. Buybacks build ETH-side liquidity before public
POTATO purchases open.

## Four ways to participate

| Path | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| Hot Potato | Grab, hold, and compete to finish the round as the final holder. |
| Recovery | Commit POTATO before a round and share its Recovery ETH by weight. |
| POTATO market | Buy or sell through the canonical pool once that direction is open. |
| Statics Operators | Register Operators and share game and swap-fee revenue by activation weight. |
