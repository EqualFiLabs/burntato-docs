Registered Statics Operators share two native-ETH revenue streams at launch:

- 15% of every second-and-later Grab; and
- 40% of the canonical pool's existing 1% swap fee.

The rewards router does not modify Statics and does not custody funds inside
Statics. It reads the Operator NFT and canonical Activation Registry, then
accounts for Burntato revenue independently.

## Weight, not NFT count

A registered Operator's reward weight is its stored activation multiplier. A wallet's approximate pool share is:

```text
wallet registered weight / totalRegisteredWeight
```

Two wallets with the same number of NFTs can have different reward shares if their activation multipliers differ.

`totalRegisteredOperators()` reports the current number of registrations. It is not “the percent of your Operators registered.”

## Prospective registration

Registration starts at the current reward index and cannot claim earlier revenue. Only the current NFT owner can register.

Higher activation weight applies prospectively after synchronization. A transfer or lower observed multiplier invalidates the registration and redistributes its unpaid whole and fractional entitlement over the remaining registered weight.

A new owner does not inherit the old registration; they register explicitly.

## Revenue with no registrations

Revenue that arrives with zero registered weight, and value that cannot be redistributed after sole-registration invalidation, becomes claimable by Burntato's current Treasury recipient.

## Independent configuration

The purchase router is fixed in the Diamond deployment binding. The hook's Operator router and share are administered by hook ownership and affect future hook fees only. Rotating away from a router does not erase claims already recorded there.
