Burntato is designed to be read directly from chain. The official mainnet
registry published at launch will identify the Diamond, swap-fee hook, Operator
rewards router, and canonical external dependencies.

## Confirm the contracts

Before signing, confirm that your wallet is connected to the supported mainnet
chain and that the application addresses match the official registry. The
critical contracts should all contain bytecode:

- the Burntato Diamond, which is also the POTATO token address;
- the Burntato swap-fee hook;
- the Operator rewards router; and
- the canonical Uniswap v4 dependencies used by the pool.

Explorer source verification makes contract code inspectable. It does not
replace reading the current game and administrative state.

## Confirm the live round

Read `currentRoundId()` and `getRound(roundId)` from the Diamond. The active
round should expose the same holder, deadline, next price, Winner pot, Recovery
pool, remaining emission, and allocation displayed by the application.

Use `Round.config` for the active rules. `protocolConfig()` describes settings
for later unsnapshotted rounds and may legitimately differ.

Also read:

- `purchasesInitialized()` to confirm play is open;
- `paused()` to confirm guarded flows are available; and
- `protocolFinalized()` to confirm whether Diamond cuts remain possible.

## Confirm the market

`canonicalPoolKey()` identifies the only supported ETH/POTATO pool. Confirm its
currencies, PoolManager, hook, zero native LP fee, and tick spacing. The market
views also expose the six launch bands, stable curve hash, position count, and
launch state.

The hook exposes the 1% bilateral fee, its Treasury recipient, the 40% Operator
share, and whether public POTATO buys are open. The public-buy gate does not
affect the protocol's buyback route.

## Confirm Operator rewards

The rewards router identifies its Burntato Diamond, Statics Operator NFT, and
Activation Registry as immutable bindings. `totalRegisteredWeight()` and
`totalRegisteredOperators()` expose the current reward denominator, while
`previewRewards(operatorId)` shows the claimable state for a known Operator.

## Confirm accounting, not just balances

Winner pots, Recovery pools, future reserves, Treasury claims, and the buyback
reserve are separate liabilities. Read their dedicated views. A raw ETH balance
alone cannot tell you which purpose the funds serve, and direct ETH transfers
do not become sponsored pots.
