Verification is a sequence of independent claims. Source verification on an explorer does not prove current roles, correct initialization, or compatibility with a frontend.

## 1. Establish provenance

- Pin chain ID and a trusted RPC.
- Read the deployment manifest and recorded source commit.
- Require nonempty bytecode at the Diamond, hook, router, and named external dependencies.
- Compare runtime code hashes where the manifest provides them.
- Confirm deployment receipts succeeded.

## 2. Inspect the Diamond

- Resolve installed selectors through the loupe.
- Confirm facet addresses match the intended release.
- Read `foundationConfigured()`, `purchasesInitialized()`, `paused()`, and `protocolFinalized()`.
- Read authority, guardian, Treasury recipient, reward allocator, and distributor state.
- Compare `protocolConfig()` with intended future defaults and active `Round.config` separately.

## 3. Inspect accounting

- Reconcile the current round, named future reserves, aggregate reserve liabilities, Treasury claims, buyback reserve, Recovery commitments, and Treasury reward escrow.
- Confirm explicit sponsorship is not conflated with automatic game allocations.
- Remember that the raw Diamond ETH balance can include untracked direct transfers.

## 4. Inspect the market

- Confirm PoolKey currencies, PoolManager, hook, tick spacing, and pool ID.
- Compare `marketCurveHash()` and position count.
- Confirm launch state and dead-address position ownership.
- Read hook owner, fee recipient, fee BPS, Operator router/share, and external-buy gate.
- Verify the Diamond's canonical hook and PoolManager bindings.

## 5. Inspect Operators

- Confirm router bindings to Burntato, the Operator NFT, and Activation Registry.
- Confirm the NFT and registry point back to the expected canonical relationship.
- Read total registered weight and count.
- Verify purchase and hook revenue route to the intended router.

## 6. Exercise representative flows

On a disposable or explicitly approved environment, prove:

- an exact-price Grab and stale-price failure;
- holder-time emission at zero, partial, and full vesting;
- expiry, settlement, Winner claim, and Recovery claim;
- allowed and restricted POTATO movement;
- canonical sell and, when enabled, buy routes;
- buyback funding and positive execution;
- Operator register, sync/invalidation, and batch claim; and
- pause activation plus authority-only unpause.

Report each layer honestly: local tests, CI, fork qualification, live readback, explorer verification, and production deployment are different evidence.
