From Grab two onward, 13% of each payment enters a dedicated POTATO buyback
reserve. Anyone can add ETH to that reserve and anyone can execute an eligible
buyback; acquired POTATO goes to the current Treasury recipient.

## Fund the reserve

Purchase allocations and payable `fundBuybackReserve()` increase `buybackReserveEth()`. Direct funding must be positive and does not execute a swap or advance the cooldown.

Funding remains available before purchase initialization, while paused, before market launch, and after Diamond finalization. A plain ETH transfer does not credit the reserve.

## Execute

`buyback()` is parameterless. After launch and cooldown, it:

1. selects at most the governed `maxSpend` slice;
2. reserves room for the configured caller reward;
3. buys POTATO exact-input through the canonical pool;
4. sends output directly to the current Treasury recipient;
5. calculates the caller reward from **actual ETH spent**; and
6. restores unspent slice value to the tracked reserve.

There is intentionally no user-supplied quote, slippage floor, deadline, or output recipient.

## Failure and partial fill

A zero-spend or zero-output execution reverts completely: no caller reward, reserve change, or cooldown consumption. A partial positive fill is valid, and the event reports gross slice, ETH spent, POTATO bought, reward, and final reserve.

## Launch limits

At launch, one execution can use at most 2 ETH, the caller reward is 0.5% of
ETH actually spent, and the delay is one block. The installed facet permanently
caps the reward rate at 1% once Diamond cuts are finalized. Setting `maxSpend`
to zero disables execution without losing accrued reserve.

The buyback path does not toggle external user buys and pays no bilateral hook fee.
