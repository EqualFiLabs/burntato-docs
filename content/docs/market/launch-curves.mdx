The reviewed launch profile reserves 100 million POTATO by default and divides it across six overlapping price bands. It creates 56 Uniswap v4 positions without requiring launch ETH.

## Allocation profile

| Band | Share of POTATO | Positions |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 2.5% | 11 |
| 2 | 7.5% | 11 |
| 3 | 12.5% | 11 |
| 4 | 20% | 11 |
| 5 | 42.5% | 11 |
| 6 | 15% | 1 |
| **Total** | **100%** | **56** |

The fixed profile uses tick spacing 60 and initial tick 170,280. `marketCurves()` exposes the canonical bands and `marketCurveHash()` provides a compact identity for deployment checks.

## Single-sided start

The initial square-root price is set to the profile's upper tick. Every position begins POTATO-sided, so `launchMarket()` is nonpayable and consumes no Treasury ETH.

ETH-side inventory arrives later through buybacks and ordinary market activity. External buys begin disabled so governance can establish liquidity before opening that direction to users.

## Permanent positions

Every position NFT is minted directly to:

```text
0x000000000000000000000000000000000000dEaD
```

The launch liquidity cannot be withdrawn or repositioned. There is no owner-held NFT, recovery key, or protocol fee in the native LP layer. The Burntato hook fee is the revenue path.

## Validation boundaries

Configuration rejects an allocation that overflows the aggregate amount domain or produces a zero or overflowing derived position. A second launch or post-launch reconfiguration reverts.

:::note Price is emergent
The bands define available liquidity, not a guaranteed future price. Buybacks, sells, external buys after enablement, and Recovery demand all change the realized market state.
:::
