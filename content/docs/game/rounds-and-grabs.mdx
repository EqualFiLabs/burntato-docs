Each round is a state machine with one current holder, one stored deadline, one next price, and its own snapshotted configuration.

## Activation

When a round activates, Burntato:

- installs its already-snapshotted terms;
- starts with the configured price and emission budget;
- applies that round's Winner and Recovery reserves;
- activates any Treasury-funded reward budget; and
- snapshots the current governed defaults for the following round.

An active round can initially be holderless. The countdown becomes meaningful after its first successful Grab.

## Exact-price purchases

`buyPotato()` accepts exactly `Round.nextPrice`. Overpayment and underpayment both revert. Integrations should simulate immediately before submission and handle a competing purchase as ordinary stale state.

After a successful purchase:

```text
increase = ceil(price * priceIncreaseBps / 10_000)
nextPrice = price + increase
```

Ceiling division guarantees a positive base-unit increase whenever `priceIncreaseBps` is nonzero.

## Diminishing reset duration

The duration uses the purchase index *before* it increments:

```text
maximumReduction = roundTimeout - minimumRoundTimeout
reduction = min(purchaseIndex * roundTimeoutDecay, maximumReduction)
resetDuration = roundTimeout - reduction
deadline = block.timestamp + resetDuration
```

With fresh defaults, successful Grabs reset to 60, 55, 50 minutes, and so on until the five-minute floor. Every later Grab resets to five minutes. A new round starts over at the initial duration.

Read the stored `Round.deadline`; do not reconstruct it from event ordering or a local clock.

## The first-purchase rule

Purchase index zero sends its entire price to the following round's Winner reserve. Later Grabs use the six-way configuration. This rule makes the next round's opening funding independent of whether the current round reaches a second purchase.

## Expiry

A Grab after the stored deadline reverts. Any account may then call `settleRound()`. Settlement and claiming are separate so no arbitrary caller can choose another user's claim recipient.
