Every round begins with an independent base-emission budget. A new holder receives a maximum opportunity calculated from what remains:

```text
maxReward = floor(remainingEmission * emissionStepBps / 10_000)
earned = floor(maxReward * min(heldSeconds, vestingDuration) / vestingDuration)
```

Only `earned` is deducted and minted. The unearned portion stays in the same round and informs the next holder's opportunity.

## What advances emission

- Elapsed holder time advances vesting.
- A later Grab finalizes the outgoing holder.
- Settlement finalizes an unresolved last holder while unpaused.
- A fully vested current opportunity can be materialized before either event.

Purchase count by itself does not consume the budget. Cycling at the same timestamp earns zero.

## Launch curve

Each round launches with a 10,000 POTATO base budget. A new holder can earn 10%
of what remains, vesting linearly over four minutes. Fully vested opportunities
therefore leave:

```text
10,000 → 9,000 → 8,100 → 7,290 → ...
```

The first holder can earn at most 1,000 POTATO. If held for two minutes, that holder earns 500 POTATO; after four minutes, the full 1,000 POTATO.

## Materializing a mature opportunity

`materializeMaturedEmission()` is permissionless once the current opportunity has fully vested. It does not change the holder, price, or deadline. The finalized flag prevents a second mint.

During the global emergency pause, new materialization and central protocol minting revert.

## Unused budget

Unused base emission is not force-minted, rolled into the next round, sent to Treasury, or added to Recovery. A new round starts with its own snapshotted budget.

Treasury reward schedules use a parallel holder-time curve but transfer pre-funded POTATO rather than minting new supply. See [Treasury rewards](/docs/potato/treasury-rewards).
