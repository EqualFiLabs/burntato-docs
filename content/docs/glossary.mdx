## Game terms

**Grab** — a successful exact-price `buyPotato()` purchase that changes the current holder.

**Holder** — the wallet currently holding the in-game Hot Potato state. This is not an ERC-721 or transferable token.

**Winner pot** — ETH claimable by the final holder after a round is settled.

**Winner reserve** — ETH assigned to a future round and moved into its Winner pot at activation.

**Recovery pool** — ETH distributed among POTATO commitments after settlement.

**Recovery reserve** — ETH assigned to a future round and moved into its Recovery pool at activation.

**Sponsorship** — the portion of a future reserve added through permissionless funding calls. Automatic game allocations are tracked separately.

**Round snapshot** — the complete configuration fixed for a round before participation under those terms.

## POTATO terms

**POTATO** — Burntato's 18-decimal, transfer-restricted ERC-20 behind the Diamond.

**Emission opportunity** — the maximum POTATO a holder can vest from the round's remaining budget.

**Materialization** — minting a fully vested opportunity before the holder changes or the round settles.

**Recovery commitment** — POTATO escrowed for `currentRoundId + 1` and normally consumed at that round's settlement.

**Treasury reward schedule** — existing Treasury-funded POTATO distributed through a future holder-time curve without minting new POTATO.

## Market terms

**Canonical pool** — the one native ETH/POTATO Uniswap v4 pool configured in the Diamond and bound to the Burntato hook.

**Hook fee** — the bilateral swap fee realized in ETH and split between Treasury and the optional Operator router.

**Buyback reserve** — tracked Diamond ETH available for permissionless protocol POTATO purchases.

**Locked LP** — the 56 launch position NFTs sent permanently to the dead address.

## Operator terms

**Statics Operator** — the external Genesis NFT whose canonical activation multiplier determines Burntato Operator reward weight.

**Registration** — a prospective binding of an Operator ID, its current owner,
and its activation weight in Burntato's rewards router.

**Sync** — permissionless reconciliation of a registration with current ownership and activation weight.
