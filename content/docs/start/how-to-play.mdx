You need ETH for a Grab and gas. You do **not** need POTATO to enter the game.

## 1. Read the round before signing

The current round shows its holder, countdown, next Grab price, Winner pot, Recovery pool, remaining emission, and your claim state. The stored onchain deadline is authoritative.

Check that your wallet is on the supported network and that the round has not expired. A purchase must send the exact displayed price; stale or approximate values revert.

## 2. Grab the Potato

A successful Grab does five things in one transaction:

1. realizes the outgoing holder's earned POTATO;
2. allocates your ETH under the round's snapshotted rules;
3. makes you the current holder;
4. starts your holder-time emission opportunity; and
5. resets the deadline and calculates the higher next price.

The first Grab is special: its full price funds the following round's Winner
pot. From Grab two onward, the mainnet launch allocation applies: 25% current
Winner, 2% next Winner, 40% Recovery, 5% Treasury, 13% buyback, and 15%
Operators.

:::note No manual token claim after a pass
When another player Grabs from you, the protocol finalizes and mints the POTATO you earned while holding. Your unvested opportunity is not minted.
:::

## 3. Decide whether to hold or pass

There is no action required while you hold. Your potential POTATO reward grows
linearly and reaches the full opportunity after four minutes.

Another player controls whether the Potato passes: they pay the new price and become the holder. You cannot force someone else to buy it from you.

If your opportunity fully vests before the round expires, anyone may call `materializeMaturedEmission()` while the protocol is unpaused. This realizes the reward without changing the holder or timer.

## 4. Settle an expired round

After the deadline, `settleRound()` is permissionless. Settlement:

- finalizes any unresolved last-holder emission;
- records the last holder as winner;
- consumes the target round's Recovery commitments;
- burns and routes committed POTATO under the snapshotted split; and
- makes Winner and Recovery ETH claimable.

Settlement does not push ETH automatically. Claims are separate transactions.

## 5. Claim

Only the recorded winner can call `claimWinner(roundId, recipient)`. A Recovery participant calls `claimRecovery(roundId, recipient)`. Both accept an explicit safe recipient and can be claimed once.

The next round can proceed independently of whether earlier claims have been collected.

## Common transaction failures

| Error | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `IncorrectPayment` | The Grab did not send the exact current price. Refresh the round. |
| `RoundExpired` | The deadline passed before the Grab landed. Settle the round. |
| `RoundNotExpired` | Settlement was attempted too early. |
| `PurchasesNotInitialized` | Burntato has not opened for play yet. |
| `ProtocolPaused` | A guarded game flow is under emergency pause. |
| `UnauthorizedWinner` | The caller is not the recorded winner for that round. |
